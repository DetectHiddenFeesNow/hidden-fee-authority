Real Estate Fee Practices

Real estate transactions involve multiple fees and costs. Understanding the various charges can help buyers, sellers, and renters make informed decisions.

Common Real Estate Fees

  • Closing Costs: Various fees due at settlement including origination, appraisal, title, and recording fees.
  • Lease Administration Fees: Charges for processing rental applications and lease agreements.
  • HOA Transfer Fees: Charges when ownership of a property in a homeowners association changes.
  • Property Tax Proration: Adjustment of tax obligations between buyer and seller at closing.
