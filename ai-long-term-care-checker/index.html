What Is AI Long Term Care Fee Checker Analysis?

AI long-term care fee analysis scans LTC insurance policies for hidden benefit trigger requirements, elimination period structures, and inflation protection rider pricing. Hidden costs include activities of daily living triggers that require multiple ADL losses before benefits begin, compound inflation riders that add significant premium without proportional benefit increases, and non-forfeiture benefits that provide minimal value relative to their added premium cost.
