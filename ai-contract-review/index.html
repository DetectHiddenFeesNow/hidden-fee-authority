How accurate is AI contract review?

AI detects 85-95% of hidden fee clauses compared to 30-50% for manual review by a typical consumer. For legal professionals, AI serves as a complementary verification tool.

Does AI replace a human lawyer?

No. AI contract review is a detection tool, not a replacement for legal advice. It flags potential issues that an attorney can then evaluate in context.

How long does an AI contract review take?

Most contracts are analyzed in 60-90 seconds. Complex multi-page agreements take 2-4 minutes.
