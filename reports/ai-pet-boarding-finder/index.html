AI Pet Boarding Fee Finder — Hidden Kennel and Sitting Charges

July 2026

An AI pet boarding fee finder analyzes kennel contracts, pet sitting service agreements, and boarding facility terms for hidden medication administration charges, holiday surcharges, pick-up/drop-off window fees, playtime add-ons, grooming requirements, and undisclosed care surcharges that inflate boarding costs.

What Is AI Pet Boarding Fee Analysis?

AI pet boarding fee analysis uses optical character recognition to scan boarding agreements, kennel contracts, and pet sitting terms for hidden charges that pet owners frequently miss. The system identifies medication administration fees charged per dose (often $5-15 each), holiday surcharges of 25-100% on peak dates, pick-up and drop-off window fees for arrivals outside narrow time windows, playtime and socialization charges that are added as mandatory extras, and grooming requirements that force pet owners to purchase overpriced facility services.

How It Works

Step 1: Upload your boarding contract, kennel agreement, or pet sitting service terms. Step 2: AI extracts daily rates, medication fees, holiday surcharges, pickup windows, and all add-on charges. Step 3: Each charge is compared against industry averages for similar facilities in the same region. Step 4: Hidden charges, mandatory add-ons disguised as optional, and fees exceeding reasonable ranges are flagged.

Types of Hidden Pet Boarding Fees AI Detects

Medication Administration. $5-15 per dose for giving pets medication, charged even for simple daily pills. AI flags rates above industry average.

Holiday Surcharges. 25-100% premium on peak boarding dates. AI identifies surcharges that apply to dates not clearly defined as holidays.

Pick-Up/Drop-Off Window Fees. Additional $25-75 for arrivals outside specific windows. AI flags unreasonably narrow windows.

Playtime & Socialization Fees. Charges for basic interaction that should be included in the daily rate. AI identifies mandatory add-ons.

Grooming Requirements. Mandatory grooming services before pick-up that cost $40-100.

Real-World Example

A pet owner in Seattle paid $65/night for boarding two dogs for 10 nights. The total quoted was $1,300. The final bill came to $1,875. AI analysis identified medication fees of $10 per dose for two dogs twice daily ($400), a Christmas holiday surcharge of 50% ($325 applied to 5 of 10 nights), and a $50 early drop-off fee for arriving at 9 AM instead of 10 AM. Hidden charges: $775.

Common Hidden Fees Detected

Service Fees. Charges added to service agreements that are not clearly disclosed in marketing materials or initial quotes, often ranging from 5-20% of the total.

Administrative Surcharges. Processing and handling fees that are pure profit for the provider, typically $10-100 per transaction.

Escalation Clauses. Annual price increase provisions of 5-25% that are buried in fine print and not disclosed during the signup process.

Auto-Renewal Traps. Contract terms that automatically renew with fees charged before the customer can cancel.
