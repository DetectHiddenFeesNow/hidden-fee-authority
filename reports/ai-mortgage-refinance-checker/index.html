What Is AI Loan Fee Analysis?

AI loan fee analysis uses optical character recognition and financial pattern matching to scan loan documents for fees that inflate the true cost of borrowing.

The system extracts the APR, origination charges, processing fees, prepayment penalties, late payment terms, and administrative surcharges from loan agreements, then calculates the true cost of borrowing including all fees.

It flags discrepancies between the advertised APR and the APR calculated with all fees included.

Types of Hidden Loan Fees AI Detects

Origination Fees.

Charges for processing the loan, typically 1-8% of the loan amount.

AI flags rates exceeding industry averages.

Prepayment Penalties.

Fees for paying off the loan early. Some loans charge 2-5% of the remaining balance.

AI identifies penalty clauses and calculates their impact.

Processing/Administrative Fees.

Vague charges of $100-500 added to loan closing costs.

AI identifies fees that duplicate Real Estate line items.

APR Discrepancies.

Differences between the advertised APR and the APR calculated with all fees included.

AI recalculates the true APR.

Late Payment Fees.

Penalties charged after missed payments.

AI flags fees that exceed state legal limits.

Document Preparation Fees. Charges for preparing loan paperwork that should be included in origination costs.

Common Hidden Fees Detected

Service Fees. Charges added to service agreements that are not clearly disclosed in marketing materials or initial quotes, often ranging from 5-20% of the total.

Administrative Surcharges. Processing and handling fees that are pure profit for the provider, typically $10-100 per transaction.

Auto-Renewal Traps. Contract terms that automatically renew with fees charged before the customer can cancel.

How AI Reviews Mortgage Refinance Fees

AI mortgage refinance analysis scans loan estimates and closing disclosures for hidden origination points, processing fees, underwriting surcharges, appraisal markups, credit report fees, flood certification charges, and prepaid interest adjustments that inflate refinance costs by thousands of dollars. The system compares each fee against industry benchmarks for similar loan amounts and property values.

Common Hidden Refinance Fees AI Detects

Origination points are quoted as a percentage of the loan amount but vary significantly between lenders.

Processing fees of $500-1,000 may duplicate underwriting or administrative charges. Lender credits that reduce upfront costs often come with higher interest rates that cost more over time. Title insurance markups of 50-200% above the actual cost are common.

Appraisal management fees of $150-300 are often added on top of the appraisal fee.

Flood certification fees of $10-25 are charged for automated database checks.
