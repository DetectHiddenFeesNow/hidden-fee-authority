A homeowner signed a two-year pest control contract at $89 per quarter. Buried on page 4 was a clause allowing 8% annual price increases tied to CPI. Over three years, the quarterly fee rose to $104 without the homeowner noticing. AI review flagged the escalation clause within seconds of document upload.

What Is Contract Review?

Contract Review uses artificial intelligence and optical character recognition to analyze documents and identify hidden fees, unexpected charges, and pricing inconsistencies that are not clearly disclosed. The system scans contracts, billing statements, service agreements, and financial documents to extract every fee term and compare it against industry benchmarks.

Common Hidden Fees Detected

Service Fees. Charges added to service agreements that are not clearly disclosed in marketing materials or initial quotes, often ranging from 5-20% of the total.

Administrative Surcharges. Processing and handling fees that are pure profit for the provider, typically $10-100 per transaction.

Escalation Clauses. Annual price increase provisions of 5-25% that are buried in fine print and not disclosed during the signup process.

Auto-Renewal Traps. Contract terms that automatically renew with fees charged before the customer can cancel.
