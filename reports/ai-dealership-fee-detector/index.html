What Is Dealership Fee Detector?

Dealership Fee Detector uses artificial intelligence and optical character recognition to analyze documents and identify hidden fees, unexpected charges, and pricing inconsistencies that are not clearly disclosed. The system scans contracts, billing statements, service agreements, and financial documents to extract every fee term and compare it against industry benchmarks.

Common Hidden Fees Detected

Service Fees. Charges added to service agreements that are not clearly disclosed in marketing materials or initial quotes, often ranging from 5-20% of the total.

Administrative Surcharges. Processing and handling fees that are pure profit for the provider, typically $10-100 per transaction.

Escalation Clauses. Annual price increase provisions of 5-25% that are buried in fine print and not disclosed during the signup process.

Auto-Renewal Traps. Contract terms that automatically renew with fees charged before the customer can cancel.

How AI Detects Hidden Dealership Fees

AI dealership fee detection analyzes vehicle purchase agreements, lease contracts, and financing documents for hidden dealer charges that inflate the cost of buying or leasing a vehicle by $1,000-5,000. The system extracts every fee line item from the purchase agreement, including documentation fees, dealer preparation charges, VIN etching costs, extended warranty pricing, gap insurance premiums, and add-on product packages. Each fee is then benchmarked against typical dealer pricing in the same geographic area and checked against state regulations that may cap certain charges.

The AI engine specifically targets the dealer practices most likely to generate hidden profit: documentation fees that range from $150-800 but cost the dealer $10-50 to process, dealer preparation fees that duplicate work already included in the vehicle's base price, VIN etching at $100-400 for a process costing $5-20, extended warranty markups of 50-200% over wholesale pricing, and gap insurance priced at double or triple the independent market rate. Add-on packages for paint protection, fabric guard, and theft etching are analyzed to determine whether they provide genuine value or are purely profit generators.

Warning Signs in Dealer Purchase Agreements

  • Documentation fees that exceed your state's average or legal maximum
  • Dealer preparation or "dealer prep" fees that are not itemized
  • VIN etching or window etching added without your explicit consent
  • Extended warranty pricing without disclosure of the wholesale cost
  • Gap insurance premiums that appear significantly above market rates
  • Add-on packages described as "already installed" that you did not request
  • Financing terms with interest rates above your pre-approved lender rate
