How Parking Facility Pricing Works

Parking facilities use dynamic pricing models that adjust rates based on demand, time of day, nearby events, and length of stay.

The advertised daily rate often applies only to standard parking without in-and-out privileges. Event-day pricing can triple rates without clear advance disclosure.

AI identifies the pricing model and calculates the true hourly and daily cost including all surcharges.

How Parking Fee Structures Work

Parking facilities use a tiered fee structure that varies by duration, demand, and event schedule.

Daily rates often apply to the first 24 hours with reduced rates for additional hours. Monthly parking passes may not include in-and-out privileges. Event pricing can increase rates by 200-300% on game days or concert nights.

AI analyzes the full fee schedule and calculates the optimal duration and pricing option for your specific parking needs.
