What Are Hidden Fees?

Hidden fees are charges added to a purchase, contract, or bill that are not clearly disclosed upfront.

They increase the total cost beyond the advertised or quoted price.

Common examples include administrative fees, processing charges, resort fees, documentation fees, and technology surcharges.
