Common Hidden Fees Detected

Service Fees. Charges added to service agreements that are not clearly disclosed in marketing materials or initial quotes, often ranging from 5-20% of the total.

Administrative Surcharges. Processing and handling fees that are pure profit for the provider, typically $10-100 per transaction.

Escalation Clauses. Annual price increase provisions of 5-25% that are buried in fine print and not disclosed during the signup process.

Auto-Renewal Traps. Contract terms that automatically renew with fees charged before the customer can cancel.

How AI Reviews Courier Service Invoices

AI courier service fee analysis scans delivery invoices for hidden surcharges.

The system extracts base delivery fees, fuel surcharges (2-8%), residential delivery fees ($3-8 per stop), signature confirmation charges ($2-5), Saturday delivery premiums, address correction fees ($12-20), and dimensional weight adjustments.

Each charge is compared against the carrier's published rate schedule.

Key hidden fees: fuel surcharges that persist even when fuel prices decline, address correction fees charged when the sender's address format differs from the carrier's database standard, dimensional weight adjustments that charge based on package volume rather than actual weight when volume exceeds a 3:1 ratio, and Saturday delivery premiums of 50-100% above standard rates.
