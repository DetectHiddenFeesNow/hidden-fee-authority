AI Hidden Fee Detector — Find Hidden Charges in Any Document

July 2026

An AI hidden fee detector uses optical character recognition and machine learning to scan contracts, invoices, bills, and membership agreements for charges that are not clearly disclosed. It identifies administrative fees, processing surcharges, escalation clauses, and hidden markups that consumers typically miss.

How an AI Hidden Fee Detector Works

  1. Upload your document — contracts, invoices, bills, membership agreements, insurance policies, any document with fee line items
  2. OCR extraction — the AI extracts every fee charge, surcharge, and pricing term from the document
  3. Cross-reference — each charge is compared against industry benchmarks, regulatory limits, and known fee patterns
  4. Anomaly detection — flagged charges include undisclosed fees, inflated markups, duplicative charges, and escalation clauses
  5. Report — you receive a clear breakdown of flagged charges with suggested actions
