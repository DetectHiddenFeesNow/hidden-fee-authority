What Is Medical Bill Review?

The system scans contracts, billing statements, service agreements, and financial documents to extract every fee term and compare it against industry benchmarks.

Common Hidden Fees Detected

Service Fees. Charges added to service agreements that are not clearly disclosed in marketing materials or initial quotes, often ranging from 5-20% of the total.

Administrative Surcharges. Processing and handling fees that are pure profit for the provider, typically $10-100 per transaction.

Escalation Clauses. Annual price increase provisions of 5-25% that are buried in fine print and not disclosed during the signup process.

Auto-Renewal Traps. Contract terms that automatically renew with fees charged before the customer can cancel.

How AI Reviews Medical Bills

AI medical bill review scans itemized hospital bills, explanation of benefits documents, and physician charges for duplicate billing, upcoding, unbundled procedures, incorrect patient information, and balance billing violations. Common Medical Billing Errors

Duplicate charges for the same service on the same day appear on 15-30% of hospital bills.

Upcoding occurs when a higher-paying procedure code is used instead of the code matching the service performed. Unbundling charges for services that should be included in a single bundled code add $100-500 per visit.

Incorrect patient demographics can result in claim denials that the patient is responsible for.
