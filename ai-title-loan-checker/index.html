Hidden Title Loan Fees AI Detects

Monthly Processing Fee. $10-50/month added to the loan balance for "account maintenance." AI flags this as interest-rate inflation.

Lien Filing Fee. $15-50 for filing the lender's lien. AI flags when this is charged multiple times.

Late Payment Fee. $15-50 per missed payment. AI flags fees exceeding state limits.

Prepayment Penalty. 5-10% of remaining balance for early payoff. AI identifies inadequate disclosure.

Loan Origination Fee. 5-15% deducted from proceeds. AI calculates APR impact.

Document Preparation Fee. $25-100 for paperwork. AI identifies duplicate charges.

Common Hidden Fees Detected

Service Fees. Charges added to service agreements that are not clearly disclosed in marketing materials or initial quotes, often ranging from 5-20% of the total.

Administrative Surcharges. Processing and handling fees that are pure profit for the provider, typically $10-100 per transaction.

Escalation Clauses. Annual price increase provisions of 5-25% that are buried in fine print and not disclosed during the signup process.

Auto-Renewal Traps. Contract terms that automatically renew with fees charged before the customer can cancel.
