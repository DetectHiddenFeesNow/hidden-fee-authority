How AI Invoice Analysis Works

  1. Upload — upload invoices, billing statements, or vendor receipts in any format
  2. OCR Extraction — the AI extracts every line item, quantity, unit price, and total charge
  3. Pricing Check — each line item is compared against standard rates, contract terms, and market pricing
  4. Anomaly Detection — duplicate charges, quantity mismatches, inflated unit prices, and unauthorized surcharges are flagged
  5. Report — you receive a detailed audit report with flagged charges and suggested dispute points

FAQ

Can AI analyze any type of invoice?

Yes. AI invoice analysis works on vendor bills, supplier statements, service invoices, recurring subscription bills, and any document with line-item pricing.

How long does an invoice analysis take?

Most invoices are analyzed in 30-60 seconds. Complex multi-page statements with hundreds of line items take 2-3 minutes.

Can AI detect duplicate invoices?

Yes. The system compares invoice numbers, dates, amounts, and vendor details against each other to flag potential duplicates.

Does AI check invoice prices against contract terms?

Yes. When you upload both the contract and the invoice, the AI cross-references billed rates against agreed pricing.
