Banking Fee Practices

Financial institutions may charge fees for account maintenance, overdrafts, ATM usage, wire transfers, and other services. Understanding how these fees are structured can help consumers make informed decisions about their banking relationships.

Common Banking Fees

  • Overdraft Fees: Charged when transactions exceed available balances. Banks may process transactions in different orders that affect fee totals.
  • Monthly Maintenance Fees: Recurring charges for account upkeep. May be waived if minimum balance or direct deposit requirements are met.
  • ATM Surcharges: Fees from out-of-network ATM usage, potentially from both the ATM operator and your bank.
  • Wire Transfer Fees: Charges for sending or receiving domestic and international wire transfers.
  • Foreign Transaction Fees: Percentage-based charges on international purchases.
