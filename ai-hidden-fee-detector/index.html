Types of Hidden Fees the AI Detects

The AI classifies hidden fees into categories: administrative and processing fees that appear in telecom bills and banking statements; auto-renewal and escalation clauses in subscription and membership contracts; material markups and change order charges in construction and home service contracts; documentation and preparation fees in auto dealership and real estate transactions; regulatory and compliance surcharges in telecom utility and insurance billing; and penalty and termination fees across most service contracts.
