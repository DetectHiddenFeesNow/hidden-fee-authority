What Is Vendor Contract Review?

Vendor Contract Review uses artificial intelligence and optical character recognition to analyze documents and identify hidden fees, unexpected charges, and pricing inconsistencies that are not clearly disclosed. The system scans contracts, billing statements, service agreements, and financial documents to extract every fee term and compare it against industry benchmarks.

Common Hidden Fees Detected

Service Fees. Charges added to service agreements that are not clearly disclosed in marketing materials or initial quotes, often ranging from 5-20% of the total.

Administrative Surcharges. Processing and handling fees that are pure profit for the provider, typically $10-100 per transaction.

Escalation Clauses. Annual price increase provisions of 5-25% that are buried in fine print and not disclosed during the signup process.

Auto-Renewal Traps. Contract terms that automatically renew with fees charged before the customer can cancel.

How AI Reviews Vendor Contracts

AI vendor contract review scans supplier agreements for hidden fees. The system extracts unit pricing, volume commitments, payment terms, auto-renewal clauses, escalation formulas, and penalty structures. Each component is benchmarked against industry averages for similar supplier categories.

Key hidden vendor fees: volume pricing that applies only to unrealistically high order quantities, minimum annual purchase commitments with no carryover for unused amounts, auto-renewal price increases of 10-25% without renegotiation, administrative surcharges of $50-500 per order added after the initial quote, and expedite or rush fees that are not disclosed in the base pricing.

Common Hidden Vendor Contract Fees

Minimum billing clauses require paying for a minimum number of units or hours each month regardless of actual usage. Auto-renewal price increases of 10-25% apply at each renewal cycle without renegotiation. Administrative surcharges of $50-500 per order are added after the initial quote. Expedite and rush fees of 25-100% of the order value are charged for faster delivery without disclosure during the quoting process. Volume pricing tiers are set at quantities that most customers never reach making the tier pricing effectively unavailable.
