About CostClarity Research

July 2026

Our Mission

CostClarity Research is an independent educational publication focused on consumer fee transparency and pricing disclosure practices. We believe informed consumers make better financial decisions.

What We Do

We provide educational resources that help consumers understand how fees are structured, where hidden charges commonly appear, and what questions to ask before signing contracts or paying bills. Our content draws on publicly available information from regulatory agencies including the FTC, CFPB, FCC, and CMS.

Independence

CostClarity Research is not affiliated with any financial institution, insurance company, telecom provider, auto dealer, healthcare provider, or other commercial service provider. We do not accept payment for editorial coverage.
