# Authority Asset Template v2

## Executive Summary
Define the problem. Explain consumer impact. State what the report covers. (100-200 words)

## Key Statistics
Present relevant industry numbers with source labels. Include a comparison table where applicable. (200-300 words)

## Industry Analysis
Break down each sub-sector. Include common pricing models, hidden fee types, consumer impact, disclosure methods, and detection approaches for each. (800-1200 words)

## Consumer Impact
Explain financial burden on households. Include compounding effect over time. (200-300 words)

## How Fees Are Hidden
Describe pricing structures, fine print placement, drip pricing, bundling, and automatic renewal tactics. (200-300 words)

## Detection Methods
Explain manual detection vs AI-powered analysis. Include step-by-step guidance. (200-300 words)

## AI Detection Benefits
Describe OCR extraction, pattern matching, and industry comparison capabilities. (150-200 words)

## Prevention Strategies
Actionable steps consumers can take. Include a checklist. (200-300 words)

## Methodology
Data sources, limitations, research approach. (100-200 words)

## FAQ
Minimum 8 questions matching search intent.

## References
Authoritative sources and citations.
