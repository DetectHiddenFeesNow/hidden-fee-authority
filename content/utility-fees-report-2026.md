## Executive Summary
Utility bills contain fees beyond usage charges including distribution fees, transmission surcharges, and regulatory compliance costs.
faq:
- What utility fees are hidden? Distribution and transmission charges beyond usage rates.
- How can AI detect utility fees? AI scans bills for surcharges beyond base rates.
