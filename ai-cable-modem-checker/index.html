AI Cable Modem Fee Checker

An AI cable modem fee checker analyzes internet service provider bills and service agreements for hidden modem rental fees, router surcharges, installation charges, equipment upgrade markups, and early termination penalties that inflate the cost of home internet service. Internet providers routinely charge $10-15 per month for modem rental — equipment that costs $60-100 to purchase outright. Over two years, that rental fee totals $240-360 for a device worth $60. Router surcharges of $5-10 per month and installation fees of $50-100 are additional costs that should be one-time or included in the service price.

AI cable modem fee analysis uses optical character recognition to scan ISP bills and service agreements for hidden equipment charges. The system identifies the specific modem and router models being rented, compares the rental cost against retail purchase prices, and calculates the break-even point where buying becomes cheaper than renting. It flags router surcharges that are added to the advertised price without clear disclosure, installation fees that duplicate labor costs already included in the activation fee, and equipment upgrade markups that charge customers for hardware the provider supplies at wholesale. The system also identifies early termination fees disguised as "equipment return" charges and billing for equipment that was returned but not credited.
