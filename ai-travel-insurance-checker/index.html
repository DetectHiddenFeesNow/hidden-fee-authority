How AI Reviews Travel Insurance

AI travel insurance review scans policy documents for exclusion clauses buried in fine print, sub-limits that reduce advertised coverage amounts, time-sensitive purchase requirements for CFAR coverage, and pre-existing condition lookback periods that disqualify most claims. The system calculates the effective coverage percentage by dividing the actual payout limits by the advertised limits.
