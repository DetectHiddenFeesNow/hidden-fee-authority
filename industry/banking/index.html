What Are Hidden Fees?

Hidden fees are charges added to a purchase, contract, or bill that are not clearly disclosed upfront. They increase the total cost beyond the advertised or quoted price. Common examples include administrative fees, processing charges, resort fees, documentation fees, and technology surcharges.

FAQ

What is a hidden fee?

A charge added to a purchase, contract, or bill that is not clearly disclosed upfront. Hidden fees increase the total cost beyond the advertised price without the consumer's full awareness.

Can AI really find hidden fees in my documents?

Yes. AI document analysis detects 85-95% of hidden charges compared to 30-50% for manual review. It can process contracts, bills, invoices, and membership agreements in under 60 seconds.

How much can I save by using AI fee detection?

The average household overpays $1,200-2,400 annually in hidden fees. AI analysis typically identifies $800-1,500 in overcharges in the first year of use.

Are hidden fees legal?

Not if they are not properly disclosed. The FTC considers undisclosed mandatory fees deceptive and has taken enforcement action against companies. Many states have specific disclosure requirements.

What types of documents can AI analyze?

Contracts, invoices, billing statements, membership agreements, insurance policies, medical bills, rental agreements, subscription terms, and any document with fee-related line items.
