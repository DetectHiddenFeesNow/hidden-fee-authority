What Is Business Contract Review?

Business Contract Review uses artificial intelligence and optical character recognition to analyze documents and identify hidden fees, unexpected charges, and pricing inconsistencies that are not clearly disclosed. The system scans contracts, billing statements, service agreements, and financial documents to extract every fee term and compare it against industry benchmarks.

Common Hidden Fees Detected

Service Fees. Charges added to service agreements that are not clearly disclosed in marketing materials or initial quotes, often ranging from 5-20% of the total.

Administrative Surcharges. Processing and handling fees that are pure profit for the provider, typically $10-100 per transaction.

Escalation Clauses. Annual price increase provisions of 5-25% that are buried in fine print and not disclosed during the signup process.

Auto-Renewal Traps. Contract terms that automatically renew with fees charged before the customer can cancel.

How AI Analyzes Business Contracts for Hidden Fees

AI business contract review analyzes B2B agreements, vendor contracts, professional service terms, and corporate supply agreements for hidden charges that increase costs beyond the negotiated terms. The system extracts every pricing component — per-unit costs, volume discount structures, minimum purchase requirements, payment terms, late payment penalties, auto-renewal clauses, and price escalation provisions — and calculates the true cost of the agreement over its full term.

The AI detection engine specifically targets business-to-business fee structures that differ significantly from consumer contracts: volume pricing that applies only to unrealistic quantity thresholds, minimum purchase commitments that force over-ordering, payment terms that effectively reduce negotiated discounts through accelerated payment requirements, and escalation clauses tied to indexes that may not reflect actual cost increases. Auto-renewal terms with rate increases of 10-25% at renewal are particularly common in business service contracts.

Warning Signs in Business Contracts

  • Tiered pricing where the lowest tier requires volumes exceeding your actual needs
  • Annual price escalation tied to indexes that historically increase faster than costs
  • Auto-renewal clauses with rate increases and notice periods shorter than 60 days
  • Minimum annual purchase commitments with no carryover for unused amounts
  • Late payment penalties that exceed 1.5% per month or include compounding provisions
  • Non-compete or exclusivity clauses that prevent competitive bidding

Real-World Example
