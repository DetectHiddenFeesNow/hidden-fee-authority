What Is AI Life Insurance Fee Analysis?

AI life insurance fee analysis extracts cost of insurance charges, premium loads, policy fees, surrender schedules, and administrative surcharges from life insurance policy illustrations and contracts. Permanent life insurance (whole, universal, variable) contains multiple fee layers that are difficult for policyholders to calculate — cost of insurance that increases annually, premium loads of 5-15%, monthly policy fees, surrender penalties that can reach 100% in early years, and administrative surcharges. AI calculates the total fee impact over policy holding periods and compares it against low-cost term alternatives.

Hidden Life Insurance Costs AI Detects

Cost of Insurance Charge. The mortality-based charge that increases annually as you age. AI extracts the COI schedule and projects 10 and 20-year cost increases.

Premium Load. A percentage of each premium deducted for commissions and expenses, typically 5-15%. AI calculates the load amount over the premium-paying period.

Monthly Policy Fee. A flat fee of $5-15 per month deducted from cash value. AI flags when this fee exceeds industry averages.

Surrender Penalty. Early withdrawal penalties that can be 100% of cash value in year one, declining over 10-20 years. AI identifies surrender schedules with excessive penalty durations.

Cost of Insurance Rate Increases. COI rates that increase at 5-15% annually. AI projects long-term cost impacts and flags policies where COI increases will make premiums unaffordable later.

Rider Markups. Accelerated death benefit, waiver of premium, or accidental death riders priced 25-50% above their actual risk cost.

Real-World Example

A policyholder in Atlanta purchased a $500,000 universal life policy with a $450 monthly premium.
