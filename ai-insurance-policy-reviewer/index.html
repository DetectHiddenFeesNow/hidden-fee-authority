AI Insurance Policy Reviewer

An AI insurance policy reviewer analyzes auto, home, health, and life insurance policies for hidden policy fees, installment charges, compliance surcharges, minimum premium requirements, and coverage cap structures that reduce the effective value of insurance coverage. Policy fees of $10-50 are standard across most carriers, and installment fees of $3-10 per payment add up to $36-120 annually for monthly payers.

AI insurance policy analysis scans declarations pages for policy issuance fees, installment billing surcharges, late payment penalties that exceed state regulatory limits, minimum premium requirements that charge a full short-rate premium even for policies canceled after a few days, and non-renewal fees that penalize policyholders for switching carriers. The system cross-references each fee against state insurance regulations and benchmarks the total policy cost against comparable coverage from competing carriers.
