What the AI Checks

Technology fees for online platforms. Lab fees for science courses. Activity fees assessed to all students. Health center fees duplicating insurance. Housing deposits and cancellation penalties. Meal plan administrative surcharges. Graduation and diploma fees not disclosed at enrollment.

Common Hidden College Fees

Technology fees of $100-500 per semester. Lab fees of $50-300 per science course. Mandatory activity and health fees. Housing application fees and deposits. Meal plan administrative charges. Graduation fees in the final semester.

How AI Analysis Works

AI extracts every fee line item from your tuition bill, identifies mandatory versus optional fees, compares each against typical ranges for similar institutions, and flags charges not clearly disclosed.
