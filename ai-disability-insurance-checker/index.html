Common Hidden Fees Detected

Service Fees. Charges added to service agreements that are not clearly disclosed in marketing materials or initial quotes, often ranging from 5-20% of the total.

Administrative Surcharges. Processing and handling fees that are pure profit for the provider, typically $10-100 per transaction.

Escalation Clauses. Annual price increase provisions of 5-25% that are buried in fine print and not disclosed during the signup process.

Auto-Renewal Traps. Contract terms that automatically renew with fees charged before the customer can cancel.

How AI Detects Hidden Disability Insurance Fees

AI disability insurance analysis scans disability income policies for hidden premium structures, elimination period traps, own-occupation definitions, and benefit reduction riders that reduce the effective value of coverage. The system extracts the premium amount, benefit percentage, elimination period length, policy definition of disability, and any riders that modify coverage. Each policy component is compared against industry-standard disability insurance terms for similar occupations.

Common hidden disability insurance fees include: premium increases tied to claims experience rather than standard rates, elimination periods that reset with each new disability, own-occupation provisions that switch to any-occupation language after 24 months without clear disclosure, and residual disability riders that require a certain income loss percentage before paying benefits. Some policies also charge administrative fees of $5-15 per month that are deducted from benefits rather than listed as premiums.
