Medical Billing Practices

Medical bills can be complex with multiple codes, adjustments, and charges. Understanding how to review these documents can help consumers identify potentially unexpected charges.

Common Medical Billing Areas

  • Duplicate Charges: The same procedure or service billed more than once.
  • Coding Variability: Similar procedures may be coded differently, affecting the amount billed.
  • Out-of-Network Charges: Higher rates for providers outside your insurance network.
  • Facility Fees: Charges for using a hospital or clinic facility separate from the provider fee.
