What Is AI Receipt Analysis?

AI receipt analysis uses optical character recognition and pattern matching to scan sales receipts, restaurant bills, and retail transaction records for fees that consumers routinely overlook. The technology identifies auto-gratuity charges added without notice, service fees disguised as administrative costs, rounding adjustments that inflate totals, technology surcharges that lack justification, and unauthorized add-ons placed into the total. AI compares each line item against expected pricing for similar purchases in the same region, flagging discrepancies that indicate overcharges.

Common Hidden Receipt Charges AI Detects

Auto-Gratuity. Restaurant bills for parties of 6 or more often include gratuity of 18-22% that is not clearly disclosed until the final bill. AI flags when auto-gratuity appears without notice.

Service Fees. Event venues, delivery services, and ticket vendors add 5-20% service fees that are described as "processing" or "handling" charges but represent pure profit.

Rounding Adjustments. Cash transactions sometimes include rounding adjustments of $0.01-0.50 per item that are not transparently calculated.

Technology Surcharges. Venues add 3-10% technology surcharges for using digital payment systems, even when the cost of accepting cards is already factored into pricing.

Minimum Charge Penalties. Some merchants add $5-15 penalties for credit card transactions under a minimum amount, which may violate card network rules.

A family of 8 dining at a Chicago restaurant received a $350 bill. Common Misconceptions

Myth 1: Auto-gratuity is optional. It is often mandatory even for small parties.

Myth 2: Technology surcharges cover processing costs. Those costs are already included in merchant pricing.

Myth 3: Receipts are too small to contain hidden fees. Restaurant bills and event receipts often have 15-25% in surcharges.

Step-by-Step Checklist

  1. Review the subtotal before tax and tip.
  2. Scan for auto-gratuity percentage applied to the pre-tax amount.
  3. Check for service fees, technology surcharges, or processing fees.
  4. Verify unit prices against menu or listed prices.
  5. Confirm the total matches the sum of line items.

How AI Analyzes Receipts

The system flags items that appear inflated, surcharges that are not clearly explained, and totals that don't match the sum of individual items.
