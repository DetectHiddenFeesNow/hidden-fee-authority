What Is AI Document Scanning?

AI document scanning is the process of using optical character recognition to digitize and analyze contracts, bills, invoices, membership agreements, and financial documents for hidden fees, unfair clauses, and unexpected charges. The system processes PDFs, images, and text files, extracting every fee line and comparing it against industry benchmarks and known hidden fee patterns.

Common Hidden Fees AI Document Scanning Detects

Administrative Fees. Charges for processing paperwork that are added to contracts without specific justification, ranging from $15-500 depending on the document type.

Escalation Clauses. Provisions allowing automatic price increases of 5-25% annually that are buried in service agreements.

Auto-Renewal Traps. Terms that automatically renew contracts with fees charged before notice periods expire.

Cancellation Penalties. Fees for canceling service that range from 30-100% of remaining contract value.

Late Payment Stacking. Compounding penalty structures that layer fees on top of unpaid fees.
