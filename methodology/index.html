How We Prepare Our Content

CostClarity Research is an independent educational publication. Our content is prepared through a systematic review of publicly available information.

Sources

We draw on information from:

  • Federal Trade Commission (FTC) — consumer protection guidance, enforcement actions, and rulemaking
  • Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) — financial product fee disclosures and complaint data
  • Federal Communications Commission (FCC) — telecommunications billing regulations
  • Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) — healthcare billing and coding resources
  • State attorneys general — consumer protection statutes and enforcement
  • Industry publications and consumer advocacy organizations

Review Process

Content is reviewed for factual accuracy before publication. We update content to reflect changes in regulations, industry practices, and available consumer protections. Where applicable, we cite specific regulatory guidance, reports, and public data.

Limitations

This website provides educational information only. It does not constitute legal, financial, or professional advice. Regulations vary by jurisdiction and may change over time. Consumers should consult qualified professionals for advice specific to their circumstances.
