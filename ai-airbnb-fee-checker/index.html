AI Airbnb Fee Checker

An AI Airbnb fee checker analyzes short-term rental receipts, booking agreements, and host service terms for hidden cleaning fees, service charges, occupancy taxes, cancellation penalties, and security deposit deductions that inflate the nightly rate by 30-70% above the advertised price. Short-term rental platforms display nightly base rates separately from mandatory fees, making it difficult to compare true costs across properties.

AI short-term rental analysis scans booking receipts for host service fees (typically 3-5% displayed as a separate line), platform service charges (10-18% charged to guests), cleaning fees ($50-200 per stay regardless of length), occupancy taxes that vary by jurisdiction and are added at checkout, and cancellation penalty structures that become stricter as the check-in date approaches. The system calculates the true nightly cost including all fees and compares it against hotel rates in the same area. It also identifies security deposit deduction patterns from host reviews and flags properties with disproportionate deposit claims.
