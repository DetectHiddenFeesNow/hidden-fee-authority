AI Health Clinic Membership Fee Checker

An AI health clinic membership fee checker analyzes direct primary care agreements, concierge medicine contracts, and health membership terms for hidden visit limits, after-hours consultation premiums, lab test markups, medication dispensing surcharges, and telemedicine restrictions that reduce the value of health clinic memberships. Direct primary care memberships charge monthly fees of $50-150 for enhanced access to primary care, but the scope of included services varies dramatically between clinics.

AI health clinic analysis scans membership agreements for visit limits that cap the number of included office visits per year (after which per-visit charges of $25-75 apply), after-hours consultation premiums that charge $50-100 for phone or video consultations outside regular business hours, lab test markups that pass through lab costs at 100-300% above the actual lab fee, medication dispensing surcharges for routine medications dispensed in-office, and telemedicine restrictions that limit virtual visits to specific conditions or times. The system calculates whether the membership fee plus any expected add-on charges is less than the cost of using traditional insurance for the same services.
