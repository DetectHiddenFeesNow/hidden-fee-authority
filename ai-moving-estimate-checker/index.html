How AI Detects Hidden Moving Charges

AI moving estimate analysis begins with optical character recognition scanning the moving contract and bill of lading to extract estimated weight, inventory list, mileage, stair fees, long-carry charges, packing material costs, fuel surcharges, and valuation coverage. The system then cross-references these charges against FMCSA regulations, the mover's published tariff, and industry pricing data for similar moves. Each charge is compared against the regulatory maximum and industry average to identify inflated or improper fees. The final report shows which charges are fair, which are inflated, and which may violate federal moving regulations.

Real-World Example

A family in Boston received a $3,800 moving estimate for a 3-bedroom home move to Philadelphia. The final bill was $6,100. AI analysis revealed the estimated weight of 7,200 lbs was actually 5,400 lbs on the certified scale — a $720 overcharge. Stair fees of $50 per floor were applied for a ground-floor condo ($200). Long-carry fees of $100 were triggered at 30 feet from truck to door. Boxes were charged at $4.75 each versus $1.50 retail ($520 overcharge on 160 boxes). A fuel surcharge of 18% was applied despite fuel prices being below the contract threshold ($558). Total hidden charges: $2,298.
