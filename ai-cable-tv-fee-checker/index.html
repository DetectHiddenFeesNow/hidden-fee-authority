What Is AI Loan Fee Analysis?

AI loan fee analysis uses optical character recognition and financial pattern matching to scan loan documents for fees that inflate the true cost of borrowing. The system extracts the APR, origination charges, processing fees, prepayment penalties, late payment terms, and administrative surcharges from loan agreements, then calculates the true cost of borrowing including all fees. It flags discrepancies between the advertised APR and the APR calculated with all fees included.

How It Works

Step 1: Upload your loan agreement, mortgage statement, or financing contract. Step 2: AI extracts APR, origination fees, processing charges, prepayment terms, and all fee line items. Step 3: The system recalculates the true APR including all fees and compares against the advertised rate. Step 4: Discrepancies, excessive fees, and improperly disclosed charges are flagged for review.

Types of Hidden Loan Fees AI Detects

Origination Fees. Charges for processing the loan, typically 1-8% of the loan amount. AI flags rates exceeding industry averages.

Prepayment Penalties. Fees for paying off the loan early. Some loans charge 2-5% of the remaining balance. AI identifies penalty clauses and calculates their impact.

Processing/Administrative Fees. Vague charges of $100-500 added to loan closing costs. AI identifies fees that duplicate other line items.

APR Discrepancies. Differences between the advertised APR and the APR calculated with all fees included. AI recalculates the true APR.

Late Payment Fees. Penalties charged after missed payments. AI flags fees that exceed state legal limits.

Document Preparation Fees. Charges for preparing loan paperwork that should be included in origination costs.
