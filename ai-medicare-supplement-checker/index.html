What Is AI Medicare Supplement Fee Analysis?

AI Medicare supplement fee analysis scans Medigap policy documents, rate schedules, and enrollment agreements for fee structures that increase costs beyond the advertised premium. Medicare supplement plans (Medigap) are standardized by letter (Plan A through Plan N), but insurance companies set their own pricing and apply different rate adjustment methods. The AI identifies attained-age rating vs issue-age rating pricing models, enrollment period surcharges, underwriting penalties for pre-existing conditions, and discount expiration structures that cause premiums to spike in later years.

How AI Medigap Fee Analysis Works

Step 1: Upload your Medicare supplement policy, rate schedule, or plan comparison documents. Step 2: AI extracts the rating method (attained-age, issue-age, or community-rated), current premium, discount structures, and rate adjustment history. Step 3: Each policy feature is compared against Medicare guidelines and industry pricing data for the same plan letter in your region. Step 4: Hidden cost drivers — like attained-age rating that increases premiums 8-15% annually after age 65 — are flagged with projected 5 and 10-year cost projections. Step 5: You receive a plan cost analysis with alternative recommendations.

Hidden Medigap Fees AI Detects

Attained-Age Rating Premium Escalation. Premiums based on your current age, increasing each birthday by 8-15% annually. AI calculates the 5 and 10-year cost projection and flags policies where attained-age rating will significantly outpace issue-age or community-rated alternatives.

Issue-Age Rating Lock-In. Premiums based on your age at purchase but still subject to annual inflation increases of 5-10%. AI separates age-based increases from general inflation adjustments.

Enrollment Period Surcharges. Higher premiums for enrolling outside the Medigap open enrollment window. AI calculates the surcharge percentage and flags whether guaranteed issue rights apply.

Underwriting Surcharge. Premium adjustments for pre-existing conditions when enrolling outside guaranteed issue periods. AI identifies underwriting questions that could trigger surcharges.

Discount Expiration. Introductory discounts that expire after 6-12 months, causing sudden premium jumps. AI calculates the post-discount premium and compares it against standard rates.

Household Discount Traps. Discounts for married couples that require both spouses to have the same carrier. AI flags scenarios where one spouse switching plans would eliminate the discount for both.

Real-World Example

A 68-year-old in Florida purchased Medigap Plan G with an attained-age rating model. The initial premium was $165/month. AI analysis projected that at age 75, the premium would reach $295/month based on the 10% annual age increase plus 6% general inflation — a 79% increase in 7 years. The same plan with an issue-age rating would reach approximately $225/month in the same period. The attained-age policy would cost over $7,200 more over 10 years.

Common Red Flags in Medigap Policies

  • Attained-age rating without clear disclosure of future cost increases
  • Introductory discounts lasting less than 12 months
  • Household discounts requiring both spouses to remain on the same carrier
  • Underwriting questions that could trigger health-based surcharges outside open enrollment
  • Rate schedules with no cap on annual premium increases
  • Plan terms that don't specify the rating methodology in plain language

FAQ

What is the difference between attained-age and issue-age rating?

Attained-age premiums increase as you get older, typically 8-15% per year. Issue-age premiums are based on your age at purchase but still increase with general inflation. Attained-age policies generally become more expensive over time.

Can AI compare Medigap plans across insurance companies?

Yes. Upload multiple plan documents and AI compares rating methods, current premiums, projected increases, and benefit structures side by side.

What Medigap documents can AI review?

Policy certificates, rate schedules, plan comparison charts, enrollment applications, and annual premium renewal notices.

Can AI detect when a Medigap discount will expire?

Yes. AI identifies introductory discount structures and calculates the post-discount premium to project the true future cost.

How much can Medicare beneficiaries save?

Beneficiaries typically save $300-1,200/year by identifying attained-age rating traps, unnecessary underwriting surcharges, and discount expiration that would otherwise increase premiums unexpectedly.

Does AI review Medicare Advantage plans too?

This tool is focused on Medigap supplement plans. Medicare Advantage plans have different cost structures and are supported in separate analysis tools.
